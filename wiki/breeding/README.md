# Breeding Program

This section is the working manual for the small breeding program. It started around the Oregon Breeding Group SBxBS01 seed packs, then narrowed toward dark-purple stabilization, and now has a longer-term black-line target after the Kalash Valley Black discovery. Background on Wayne / OBG / what BS01 is lives in [`concepts/oregon-breeding-group.md`](../concepts/oregon-breeding-group.md); the rationale for launching the program lives in [the launch decision](../decisions/2026-04-26-breeding-program-launch.md); the Kalash Valley Black pivot lives in [the 2026-05-31 decision](../decisions/2026-05-31-kalash-valley-black-target.md). The pages here are the *protocol* — how we actually do it.

## Goals (in priority order)

1. **Breed toward an actual black cannabis line** — Kalash Valley Black is now the priority outside parent target because it is described as maturing to deep black, with sweet/berry/floral aroma and landrace resilience.
2. **Use elite purple/fruity Serious Black expression as the bridge parent** — the current SBxBS01 hunt still matters because A/D-style purple Serious Black plants may supply fruit, bag appeal, and known local performance.
3. **End at repeatable feminized seed production** — the mature system should produce large feminized batches where progeny tests show >90% on-target female offspring.
4. **Bank pollen from selected SBxBS01 males** — multi-year supply in dedicated freezer, useful either for the original purple-line work or for a reciprocal Kalash Valley Black cross.
5. **Progeny-test family lots instead of trusting one-off color** — the F2/F3+ generations reveal whether black/purple expression, terps, structure, and resilience are actually passing forward.

What we are explicitly **not** doing: commercial-scale production or broad BS-line exploration as the main path. Full IBL-level stabilization still takes 4–6+ generations, but the program is now intentionally pointed in that direction.

## Two parallel tracks

The program runs as two physically separated tracks because pollen contamination of the main flower tent would destroy the smokable harvest:

- **Track A — pollen production.** Start all 10 SBxBS01 *regulars* now. Veg only long enough to make sturdy sex/pollen plants, then flip the batch to 12/12, cull females as soon as they declare, evaluate males (see [male-evaluation.md](male-evaluation.md)), collect separately labeled pollen, aliquot, and freeze. Total time germ → frozen pollen: ~7–8 weeks. Track A is the *speed track*. Current status: 10 regular seeds were started around 2026-04-28; 7 sprouted, 2 died during transplant to coco coir, and 5 active plants remain as R1-R5. Track A flipped to 12/12 on 2026-05-24. R1 and R4 have confirmed male pre-sex organs / male sex expression as of 2026-05-30; evaluate them against [male-evaluation.md](male-evaluation.md) before pollen collection. The grow-scoped plant record is [Track A pollen run](../grows/breeding-track-a-2026-04-28/README.md).
- **Track B — phenotype hunt / stabilization selection.** Pop the next candidate population after the current grow harvests only if it still supports parent selection before Kalash Valley Black is available. Veg small, clone promising females before flip, flower out, score against the target, and preserve only plants/families that can support the black-line goal. Track B is the *selection track*.
- **Track C — Kalash Valley Black acquisition and parent run.** Kalash Valley Black cannot be assumed available to the project before September 2026 because of the reported United States shipping pause. Once sourced, run it as a serious parent population: identify black-color donors, preserve useful females, evaluate males separately, and make known-family SBxBS01 × Kalash Valley Black crosses.

Once a keeper is identified, revive her clone, flower her in isolation, and pollinate one or two branches with the best available known pollen. Before Kalash Valley Black arrives, that may mean SBxBS01 pollen for the original purple-line work. After Kalash Valley Black arrives, the priority cross becomes an elite purple/fruity SBxBS01 parent with a Kalash Valley Black parent. Feminized seed production comes later, after a family proves it can pass the target forward at high percentage.
