# Daily Log - 2026-06-12 (Day 90 / Flower Day 40)

**Time:** user reservoir follow-up  
**Stage:** Late Flower - Day 40 of 12/12

## User Report

The main plants recently showed broad stress: burnt leaf tips and leaves getting lighter green. Reservoir EC had been much too high, around or above 2.0 mS/cm. The reservoir was run for roughly 24 hours on pH-adjusted tap water after the 2026-06-10 reset. Nutrients were then added back today; the user initially considered EC 1.2 mS/cm but was concerned that tips still looked burned and the plants looked lighter.

## Assessment

The visible burnt tips should not be used as the only signal because already-damaged tips will not heal, and plants can look worse for a few days after a high-EC event. Judge recovery by new growth, fresh tip/edge burn, posture, tray cycling, and root-zone evidence.

AutoPot-specific guidance supports conservative reservoir strength because nutrients are continuously available in the trays. General greenhouse EC guidance also treats excessive soluble salts as a root-zone osmotic stress issue; a true flush is useful when root-zone EC is still high, but simply extending plain water in the reservoir may not remove salts already concentrated in the XL pots.

## Plan Filed

1. Hold the Autopot reservoir at a conservative recovery feed of about EC 0.8-1.0 mS/cm total, including tap background, for the next 48-72 hours. If the reservoir is already at EC 1.0, hold there. If it is still at EC 1.2 and symptoms are advancing, dilute toward EC 1.0 rather than feeding stronger.
2. Keep pH adjusted near the normal coco/Autopot target, around pH 5.8, and keep reservoir temperature in the normal cool range when possible.
3. Do not start a longer plain-water reservoir flush by default. Continue with light feed unless new growth keeps burning, leaves keep fading rapidly, posture declines, or root-zone/runoff/slurry evidence shows EC is still high.
4. If fresh damage keeps advancing, do a real root-zone reset instead of only extending reservoir plain water: turn off the valve, remove pots from trays, top-flush each pot drain-to-waste with pH-adjusted water until runoff EC comes down, let pots drain fully, clean trays/valves, then restart at about EC 0.6-0.8 and step up slowly.
5. Because this is a 25-gal reservoir, avoid mixing the whole tank hot during recovery. Mix smaller volumes or dilute aggressively enough that corrections remain easy.

## Watch Items For Next Agent

- Photograph or mark fresh tips and compare only new damage over the next 48-72 hours.
- Confirm tray fill/drawdown/refill behavior, especially Plant A after RS485 cutover and the watched Autopot reconnection.
- Plants B-D have no trusted current moisture probe after capacitive-node retirement; use hand checks, media smell/weight, tray behavior, and plant posture.
- Move back toward the low end of the normal late-flower band only after new growth and posture are stable.
